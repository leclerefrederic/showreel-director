# UI2: clear scene rows, three switches, Delete scene, a real lock (app 0.13.0, skill untouched)

## Step 0 (read only, before building)

a) **Review model.** Stored per scene: `status` wip, validated, locked, ditched, and an `archived` flag; the note holds the MODIFY text. Set by `SceneEdit(status:)` (OK = validated, MODIFY = wip with a note, DITCH = ditched, which archives and switches the scene off), `.sceneLock`, `.sceneEnabled`, `SceneEdit(archived:)`. The Go gate is `goBlocker` (Storyboard.swift) and the checks "Scene review" (`BuildRunner.readiness`) and `review.all` (`Checks`), all reading `SceneState.isReviewed` (validated or locked). Clap could call update_scene and add_scene with status wip/validated/ditched and archived, lock_scene and enable_scene.
b) **The lock.** `ProjectStore.plan()` calls `lockRefusal` (via `scenesChanged`): content, look, length, edge moves, asset links and any scene edit were refused; the place in the montage, the visibility and the transitions were allowed. Starts come from `ShowreelFile.timeline()`: the sum of the lengths of the enabled scenes before, unless an item holds a manual start or end; a ripple resize moves the later starts.
c) **Look.** `effective(key, for:)` is the scene's own override, else the project's; the moods and the effects pack are the project's only. `LockedLook.warning` lists the locked scenes that inherit a changed key on the card ("apply anyway"). A copy fits in the scene (`lockedLook`, new optional key, unknown keys survive).
d) **Archived and ditched.** `archived` scenes leave `timeline()`, `orderedLibrary` and the film; ditched means archived. "Not in the storyboard" listed switched-off scenes and an Archived group with "Bring back". All of it maps to hidden scenes.
e) **Existing projects, no migration.** A locked scene that is not validated is Ready (`isReviewed` includes locked); validated is Ready; a wip scene with a note is To review with that note as the instruction; archived and ditched are hidden scenes with a note "Was archived/ditched". A scene locked before UI2 has no look copy and keeps following the project's look (and the card still lists it) until it is unlocked and locked again. The stored words did not change.

## What was built, point by point

1. **One Status function** `SceneStatuses.status` (ShowreelKit/SceneStatus.swift): the six states in the order asked, the first is the icon, the tooltip lists every state that applies with its reason and the way out ("Out of date, locked: unlock it to rewrite it"); edited by hand is a line, not a state; Ready only when the file is up to date; a hidden scene has only its review state. The list, the strip's frames, the Overview and the inspector header all draw `SceneStatusIcon`; `SceneBadges`, `BuildIndicator`, `SceneStateBadge`, `MarkLayout` and `SceneIcons` (UI1) are removed.
2. **Rows and lines.** `StoryboardList.row` (ID, name as title, first words under it, duration rounded to 0.1 s), `StoryboardList.text` ("Flip card · 0.55 s", "Cut", no "to"), a glyph by type (scissors for a cut), tooltip "S01 to S02", marks kept. The view: fixed-width columns, Status read-only, the eye and the lock as buttons with tooltips and accessibility labels, a connector line, a "?" popover legend (`SceneStatuses.legend`).
3. **Switches and Delete.** `SceneSwitches` (visible, ready, locked, delete) gives the operation or the reason and the way out; the inspector's "State" section has the three switches, the instruction field (the scene's note) and "Delete scene…" with a confirmation that names what is lost (`deletionLosses`). Delete is `.deleteScene`, one typed operation: the scene, its montage items and transitions, its links in the manifest; its ID goes to `retiredIDs` (never reused); Undo restores all of it; journalled as "deleteScene". OK, MODIFY, DITCH, Archive and the badge menu are gone from the list, the strip and the context menus ("Remove from film" became "Hide", "Archive" became "Delete scene…").
4. **A real lock** (one control point: `plan()` → `lockRefusal` for what touches the scene, and `finish()` → `lockShiftRefusal` for a change elsewhere, because only there the new file is known). Refused: content, look, length, visibility, name of its elements, deletion, any move, and any change that moves its start or, for a hidden scene, what is before it: "S07 is locked at 0:24: unlock it, or change a scene after it". `BuildJob.decide`: a locked scene is never written (a locked scene with no file stays an empty frame and is listed on the card: "Locked: S02 · never rewritten by a Build (nor Generate, nor Fix this scene)"); Generate and Fix keep refusing it. **Locking takes a copy** of the effective palette, fonts, background, mood, moods and effects pack; `effective()` reads it; `LockedLook` no longer lists such a scene on the card; unlocking drops it, and the unlock card says what will change when the copy differs from the project's look.
5. **Go gate and checks.** "Every visible scene is Ready" (a locked scene is Ready, a hidden one ignored); the details name the scenes not Ready. The Go is still only the user's button.
6. **Clap.** tools.json: `set_ready` and `delete_scene` added (always a card, even with apply-directly: `operate(forceCard:)`), `status` and `archived` removed from add_scene and update_scene (and ignored if sent), descriptions of update_scene, lock_scene and enable_scene rewritten. `addendum.md` and `capabilities.json` updated. The skill's portability row only says "scene review in a tabbed inspector" and stays true, so the skill is untouched and unreleased.

## Decisions taken

- The "instruction field" is the scene's existing `note` (labelled Instruction); writing it does not switch Ready by itself.
- IDs of deleted scenes are kept in a new optional `retiredIDs` key of showreel.json (older apps ignore it and keep it).
- A locked scene's own transition stays editable (not in the list of refusals).
- A change that trades time between two neighbours without moving a locked scene's start (a cut moved before it) is allowed; the one that touches the locked scene's own edges is refused.
- Undo: the unlock also undoes with the copy.
- "Lock all" (short label) locks every Ready scene.
- During my work a command of mine included `git status` by mistake; the harness refused it and nothing ran. No git command was run.
