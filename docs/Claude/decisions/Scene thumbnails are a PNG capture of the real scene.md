# Scene thumbnails are a PNG capture of the real scene

A scene's thumbnail is a PNG captured from the real rendering of the scene, not the schematic drawing of W4; owner choice of 2026-11-10.

**Why.** The schematic drawing of W4 is not what the film shows. The owner chose, among no thumbnail, a PNG of the real scene and an animated GIF, the PNG; a GIF may come later.

**What it affects.** The storyboard strip (its frames) and the scene inspector show the PNG; the viewer shares the W4 drawing code and keeps it before a build (see [[phases/W4b/report]]).
