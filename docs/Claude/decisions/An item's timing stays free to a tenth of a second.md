# An item's timing stays free to a tenth of a second

**Decision.** The moment an item appears and disappears stays free, to a tenth of a second, and does not snap to the beats and bars of the music grid. Snapping may come later as an option, off by default, once a track is analysed in the project.

**Why.** The owner accepted the recommendation of QUESTION-014, 2026-11-11.
