# The left panel splits two thirds Storyboard and one third Resources

**Decision.** "The split is 2/3 and 1/3 by default (`LibrarySplit.standard`), the divider range 25 to 85 percent." The report lists it among the decisions the owner can reverse; the proportions were never judged on a laptop screen.

**Superseded.** The default split is now the height of the Film strip: [[The Resources section takes the height of the Film strip by default]] (DECISION-023, built in RS1). The 2/3 and 1/3 stays only at full-width Film placement; the 25 to 85 percent range stands.
