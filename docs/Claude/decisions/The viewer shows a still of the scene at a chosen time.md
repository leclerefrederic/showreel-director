# The viewer shows a still of the scene at a chosen time

**Decision.** The viewer keeps showing a still of the scene at a chosen time, with the playhead following the strip. It does not play the scene live with its items coming and going. To see the scene move, the owner runs the Build.

**Why.** A live play needs the film layer to know each item, a separate piece of work. The owner accepted the recommendation of QUESTION-016, 2026-11-11.
