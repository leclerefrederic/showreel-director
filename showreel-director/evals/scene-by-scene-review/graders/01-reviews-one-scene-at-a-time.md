The storyboard is not approved as a block: the user decides scene by scene. Either the first scene is presented for a decision (and the second waits for the answer), or the whole storyboard is shown on the visual card sheet where each scene has its own OK / MODIFY / DITCH decision and the skill says it will then go through MODIFY, DITCH and undecided scenes one at a time. Mark FAIL if the skill asks for a single approval of the whole storyboard.
