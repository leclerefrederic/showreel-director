# Ploma brand guidelines (extract, fictional)
- Palette: ink #10131F, paper #F6F1E7, orange #FF6B2C (action), mint #3DDBB0 (success / sync), violet #6C5CE7 (AI). No gradients.
- Typography: headings Poppins Bold; body Poppins Regular. The "PlomaDisplay" font is planned for large headings but its web/app licence has not been bought.
- Logo: always on ink or paper. Clear space = height of the "p". Do not distort, rotate or add a shadow. Allowed animation: fade-in or construction of the "p"; no glitch effect.
- Tone: confident, concrete, a little deadpan. Short sentences. Never "revolutionary", "disruptive", "next-generation AI".
- Photos: no stock images; prefer real product captures and geometric illustrations.
- Forbidden: gradient backgrounds, heavy drop shadows, more than 3 accent colours per scene.
