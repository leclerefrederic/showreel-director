# Script examples (principles, not styles)

Three short, deliberately different sequences showing the same principle: the text gives a cue, the image answers, a short reaction punctuates; never explain what the image already shows. They illustrate voice and rhythm only. Do not reuse their tone unless the brief asks for it; write the user's own voice.

**Dry and understated (scene on a product launch)**
- "It was a quiet launch." (small type, a single dot appears)
- "Then the dot got friends." (dots multiply, the grid fills)
- "A small crowd, by now." (aside, small, after the grid fills)

**Loud and fast (scene on speed)**
- "BEFORE: 3 DAYS." (slam, red)
- "NOW: 12 MIN." (slot roll to the new value, flash on the beat)
- "GO." (one word, scale explosion)

**Institutional and calm (scene on trust)**
- "Every record, in one place." (fade, left aligned)
- "Auditable. Traceable. Yours." (three words on three beats)
- "Source: internal study, 2026." (footnote, held 4 s)

What the three share: one idea per scene, rhythm by contrast (small versus large, slow versus sudden), the longest line held longest, the reaction line shortest.
